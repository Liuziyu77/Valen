## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: a finished primary logo for Valen, an open-source multimodal System One decision model, to be placed at the top of a GitHub README.
Primary request: make a bold, completely new identity, not a minor revision of a conventional V-with-speed-lines logo. The name honors biologist Leigh Van Valen's Red Queen hypothesis: continual evolution is necessary just to keep pace. Express continual adaptation, rapid perception and decisive movement through a memorable abstract symbol.
Creative direction: "the evolving aperture". A compact asymmetrical loop formed by two interlocking, broad, smooth curved shapes, like an aperture caught mid-turn. One arc tapers and passes through the opening of the other, suggesting pursuit and perpetual renewal. A precise open notch creates forward momentum. The central negative space should subtly suggest a V without drawing a literal V. Make the symbol striking and readable at favicon scale. Use a single simple silhouette, generous negative space, deliberate proportions. Think refined research-lab identity, not esports or automobile branding.
Wordmark: exact text "Valen" with uppercase V and lowercase alen. Draw elegant contemporary geometric sans-serif lettering with a single-storey a, softened corners and medium weight; highly legible, distinctive and calm. No italics or heavy athletic lettering.
Composition: horizontal lockup, symbol on the left and wordmark on the right with thoughtful optical spacing. Roughly 3:1 canvas, centered with modest even padding, fills the canvas usefully. The symbol height approximately 1.15 times the capital-letter height. One finished logo, not multiple options.
Palette: deep graphite #303740 for the wordmark and main curved shape, with one desaturated wine-clay #986F76 curved shape as the Red Queen allusion. Quiet, low-saturation, flat solid fills. No vivid red. The wine-clay shape can be substantial enough to make the symbol distinctive, balanced against the graphite.
Backdrop: genuinely transparent PNG background with alpha; clean sharp antialiased edges. No background panel, no painted checkerboard.
Constraints: radically depart from a crown or winged V. NO crowns, royal ornaments, feathers, wings, speed streaks, lightning, DNA helices, neural-network nodes, literal eyeball, chess piece, queen figure, infinity sign, gradients, glow, shadows, metallic rendering, 3D, textures, decorative underline, slogans, extra text or watermarks. Do not imitate an existing company logo. Only the abstract emblem and the word "Valen".
